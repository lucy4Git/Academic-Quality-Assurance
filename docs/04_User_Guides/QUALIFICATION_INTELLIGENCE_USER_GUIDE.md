# Qualification Intelligence — User Guide

## What is it?

The Qualification Intelligence tool is an internal advisory calculator that helps QA Officers, Programme Coordinators, and Lecturers:

- Calculate GPA and CGPA from subject marks
- Convert percentage marks to grade points (4.0 scale)
- Summarise total credits earned
- Get an advisory NQF level mapping based on credit totals and qualification type
- Generate an advisory report with warnings and recommendations
- Export results as a CSV for record-keeping

**This tool is advisory only.** Results do NOT constitute official SAQA evaluations or formal academic credential assessments.

---

## Who can use it?

All authenticated staff members (QA Officers, Deans, HoDs, Programme Coordinators, Lecturers). Students do not have access.

Navigate to **Qualification Intelligence** in the left sidebar under **AI ASSISTANT**.

---

## Using the Calculator

### Step 1 — Fill in student details

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Student name | Full name of the student (optional but recommended for records) |
| Institution | Institution name (e.g. TUT, UP) |
| Programme name | Name of the degree/diploma programme |
| Qualification type | Select from Higher Certificate → Doctoral Degree |
| Academic year | e.g. 2024 |

### Step 2 — Enter subject marks

For each subject or module on the transcript:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Subject | Module or subject name |
| Credits | Credit value (e.g. 16) |
| Mark % | Percentage mark (0–100) |
| Semester | Semester number (1, 2, 3, ...) |

Click **+ Add subject** to add more rows. Remove any row with **Remove**.

### Step 3 — Calculate

Click **Calculate**. The results panel on the right shows immediately.

---

## Understanding Results

### Summary cards
- **GPA** — Grade Point Average on a 4.00 scale. Colour coded: green (≥3.7), blue (≥3.0), amber (≥2.0), red (<2.0).
- **CGPA** — Cumulative GPA weighted across semesters.
- **Total Credits** — Sum of all subject credits.
- **Pass Rate** — Percentage of subjects passed (≥50%).

### Advisory NQF Mapping
Blue panel showing the advisory NQF level for the qualification type selected. Includes credit gap if the total credits are below the minimum for that level.

### Warnings
Red panel listing any issues: failed subjects, low GPA, credit shortfall, or large GPA variation between semesters.

### Recommendations
Green panel with actionable advisory recommendations based on the results.

### Subject Table
Full breakdown per subject showing letter grade, grade points, quality points, semester, and pass/fail status. Failed subjects are highlighted with a red background.

## Grade Scale

| Percentage | Grade | GPA Points |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| 80–100% | A | 4.0 |
| 75–79% | A- | 3.7 |
| 70–74% | B+ | 3.3 |
| 65–69% | B | 3.0 |
| 60–64% | B- | 2.7 |
| 55–59% | C+ | 2.3 |
| 50–54% | C | 2.0 |
| 45–49% | C- | 1.7 |
| 40–44% | D | 1.0 |
| 0–39% | F | 0.0 |

Pass mark: **50%** (HEQSF standard).

| Honours / Postgrad Diploma | 8 | 120 |
| Master's Degree | 9 | 180 |
| Doctoral Degree | 10 | 360 |

## Saving and Exporting Records

### Save a record
After calculating, click **Save Record**. The record is saved under your account and appears in the **Saved Records** tab.

### Export as CSV
In the Saved Records tab, click **Export CSV** to download a formatted CSV including:
- Student and programme details
- Subject breakdown table
- Advisory summary, warnings, and recommendations
- Full advisory disclaimer

### Delete a record
In the Saved Records tab, click **Delete** next to any record. This permanently removes it.

## Important: Advisory Disclaimer

Every result and export includes this disclaimer:

> **ADVISORY ONLY** — This report is generated by the AQAA Qualification Intelligence tool for internal advisory purposes. It does NOT constitute an official SAQA qualification evaluation, recognition of prior learning determination, or any formal academic credential assessment. Contact SAQA or the relevant Quality Council for official evaluations.

Never share results with students as official determinations. For official evaluations, direct students to SAQA (www.saqa.org.za) or the relevant Quality Council (CHE, QCTO, Umalusi).
